How we coordinate staffing

Worker screening, tools and PPE

We assess experience, review employment documentation and verify the skills required for each role. Each worker brings basic hand tools such as a hammer, pry bar, utility knife, tape measure and pliers.

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Safety footwear
  • Hard hats
  • Full-face respirators
  • Half-face respirators
  • Safety glasses
  • Work gloves

We agree with the client in advance on personal protective equipment, power tools and consumables.

Training by assignment

We verify each worker’s current credentials before assignment.

  • Fall Protection
  • WHMIS
  • Confined Spaces
  • Asbestos abatement and mould remediation training as required by the client

What we confirm before work starts

  • Crew & schedule

    Crew size, tasks, shifts, start date and expected duration.

  • Tools, PPE & training

    What each party supplies and what training the assignment requires.

  • Site coordination & hours

    Your site lead, your designated Cluster contact and who approves timesheets. We coordinate available replacements with you.
